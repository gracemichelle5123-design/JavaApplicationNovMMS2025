### *STEP-BY-STEP: HOW TO RUN & USE THE SCHOOL SYSTEM*

#### *STEP 1: SETUP FILES*
1. Create folder: `SchoolManagementSystem`
2. Inside it create: `src\school\model\` and `src\school\main\` and `bin\`
3. Copy all 8 files I sent into them:
SchoolManagementSystem/
├── bin/
└── src/
    └── school/
        ├── model/
        │   ├── Gender.java
        │   ├── StudentLevel.java  
        │   ├── Course.java
        │   ├── Department.java
        │   ├── Person.java
        │   ├── Student.java
        │   └── Teacher.java
        └── main/
            └── Main.java
#### *STEP 2: COMPILE*
Open CMD in `SchoolManagementSystem` folder and run:
javac -d bin src\school\model\*.java src\school\main\Main.java
If no error shows, you’re good.

#### *STEP 3: RUN*
java -cp bin school.main.Main
